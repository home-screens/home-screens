## New in this RC

- Text size is now one slider on every module, read as a percent of the module's normal size: on modules that fit their text to their box, 100% is the fitted size and you can scale it up or down from there; everywhere else it is a percent of the module's base size. Existing walls keep rendering exactly as before, and the slider now reaches from 10% to 450%.
- Fixed modules not sizing their text to their box: every weather view had been stuck at the default size no matter how big its box, and the news module had been the same way for several releases. Text is also re-measured once the real font finishes loading, so nothing on a Raspberry Pi is laid out in the wrong face.
- The multi-month calendar can now fill its card instead of leaving the bottom half empty in a large box. New calendars do this by default; existing ones get a "Fill the space" toggle.
- Added a Wide strip view to the fullscreen weather module for a band across the foot of a landscape screen, and fixed today's high and low showing the wrong numbers after sunset on NOAA and Environment Canada feeds.
- The clock module gets an Hour format setting: follow the display's time format, 12-hour, or 24-hour. New clocks follow the display; existing clocks keep the choice they had.
- The display's 12/24-hour setting now reaches sunrise and sunset, moon phase, the fullscreen photo clock and Todoist due times, which had ignored it. Sunrise and moon also format times in the display's language.
- The Style text colour now reaches every part of a module: sports and standings scores, the analog clock face, divider lines, and the rules, rings, tracks and pills that stayed white on a light card with dark text.
- Style controls that could not affect a module are hidden instead of offered: the display control widget's Style section and the sticky note's background and text colour (it paints from its own Note colour). The Text size help text now says whether the selected module fits its text to its box.
- The rotation progress line is centered under the screen dots instead of hanging off the active dot, and it stays in step with the screen timer through re-renders. Displays can now override the progress line setting individually.
- Fullscreen photo theme overrides now reach the loaded photo too: letterbox bars and the clock overlay take the theme's colours instead of hardcoded black and white.
- A kiosk running on the hub itself now prints the hub's network address in the setup watermark and empty states, instead of a localhost address no phone could reach.
- Fresh installs no longer start on a weather provider that needs an API key; they default to Open-Meteo.
- Calendar link fetching is hardened against unsafe redirects and oversized downloads, with a per-feed "Home network" opt-in for households running their own calendar server.
- Fullscreen news gets a per-module theme override and names the feeds that did not answer.
- Added GitHub and Discord links below Help & docs in the editor settings sidebar.
- Editor polish: expanding panels no longer scroll the contents under your pointer mid-animation; undo and redo no longer fire behind an open dialog or menu; dialogs trap keyboard focus properly; modules no longer flash at the wrong size on their first frame; the air quality setup card points at the page where its key actually lives; weather providers are named consistently everywhere; backup, credential and OneDrive sign-in errors read as sentences instead of codes; and the kiosk's screen arrows are translated in every locale.
- Fixed the chores and remote pages briefly showing raw placeholder text while translations load.
- Updated a dependency to clear two published security advisories.

## Fixed

- Weather views and the news module rendering at the default text size no matter how large their box.
- Fullscreen weather showing the night's low as today's high after sunset on NOAA and Environment Canada feeds.
- Undo and redo firing on the canvas while a dialog or menu was open.
- Raspberry Pi image never starting its kiosk screen on first boot.
- Chore chart Board view squeezing everyone into one row of truncated columns on a busy day.
- Remote's Photos tab showing an empty state for households using the windowed photo module.
- History module failing to load due to upstream rate limiting.
- Module staying deselected after finishing a resize drag in the editor.
- Star chart marking future days as missed in the evening.
- Fullscreen calendar week titles breaking when a week crosses a month or year boundary.
- News module dropping stories that share an ID across different feeds, and blocked-word filtering matching partial words (like hiding "Warsaw" because of "war").
- Cross-origin write requests to the API are now rejected for security.
- A dependency update clears two published security advisories.
