## New

- Add, change and remove calendar events from the phone's new Calendar tab and from a touch wall, saved straight to Google or iCloud, repeating events included.
- Give any Google or iCloud calendar your own name and color, used everywhere that calendar shows. Thanks to MG296 for the contribution.
- A Calendars sheet on the wall: tap the color key to hide a calendar from that wall or try a new color, and set a calendar order that decides which events keep a row on a crowded day. Thanks to MG296 for the contribution.
- Switch a calendar's view or show just one person's calendar from a touch wall, and it goes back to its usual look a minute after the last touch.
- Plan meals from the wall: put a recipe on the menu from its card, or tap an empty meal to pick one from the recipe book, each with Undo.
- Add to lists from the wall: to-do cards and the grocery list get an Add button that opens an on-screen keyboard, with the accented letters each language needs.
- Draw or type a note on the wall from the Notes card, then say who it is from and how long it stays up.
- Quick Timer module: tap a length to start a kitchen timer on the wall, with the countdown and its buttons right in the box. Thanks to MG296 for the contribution.
- Timer buttons on the wall: tap a running timer for Pause, a minute more or less, mute, next step and a held Stop, and start a routine or quick timer from a new Timers button in Display Control.
- An optional grown-up code: pick the jobs that need it, such as spending tickets, unticking a chore or changing the menu, and the wall and the kids' pages ask for a short code first.
- Automatic backups on a schedule, kept on the hub and optionally sent to a shared network folder or WebDAV storage, with a private download link for the newest copy.
- Live Photos: Photo Slideshow and Full-Screen Photo can play the moving half of an iPhone Live Photo once, twice or on a loop. Thanks to MG296 for the contribution.
- Tap a card to open its screen: an open button can take a tap anywhere on the card, and photo cards show back, pause and next on a tap or move along with a swipe.
- Each wall browser is now let in on its own instead of reading the display key from the page. Walls you already have move over by themselves, and on a hub with a password a new browser waits until you let it in from Settings > Displays.
- A Famous paintings subject with 325 well-known works brings the museum collection to over 5,900 paintings, and the Artist filter now takes full names and several artists.

## Improved

- Calendar views catch up with each other: week numbers on more views, start times in the Month grid, a switch to hide places, name tags on all-day bars, day notes in the day sheet, and Multi-Month marks days with events in their calendars' colors.
- Weather: the alert banner leads with the most serious warning, wind shows gusts and direction, the Almanac adds the next moonrise or moonset, the Rain Map gets a light map, and switches a source cannot fill are hidden.
- Chores and rewards: bonus chores show on the Board and Compact views, the store marks the reward someone is saving for, the phone shows the week's stars and streak, and the redeem question says how many tickets will be left.
- Meals: family ratings show on the small Recipes list and in the editor, and Rate Dinner is easier to reach on the wall and the phone.
- Photo Slideshow gets slide and zoom transitions, the Ken Burns effect and a Shuffle order, and a library folder plays in name order.
- Full-Screen News can mark new stories and turn off the Just in tag, and a front page with several feeds gets its own title.
- Smaller modules: the sports ticker shows live game clocks, long standings page through, finance numbers follow your language, crypto gets a chart look, the Icon module takes emoji and your own pictures, and the Digital, Flip and Dot matrix clocks get a Show AM/PM switch.
- To-do and Todoist lists fit their box with an "and N more" line, and every list keeps one of each item, so adding something already there says so and a ticked one comes back.
- Times of day are written the way each language writes them, such as 08.05 in Danish.
- Important Sticky notes get a red band across the top and stay on their writer's paper.
- The editor, the phone and the wall share one set of buttons, sheets and messages: things you built ask before they are deleted, a single to-do item or day note goes at once with Undo, and sheet buttons ignore an accidental double tap.
- Editor: profile schedules use the same week strip as modules, Escape closes one window at a time, and each module offers only the settings its chosen view uses.
- Backups & data settings are grouped into four clear panels, and a backup that cannot be restored says why.
- The hub keeps its time zone data current with each update, and the System page shows which version it has.
- A batch of wording and layout tidy-ups across the editor, the phone and the wall.

## Fixed

- Security fixes: plugin downloads that hold links or point back at the hub are refused, passwords are checked one at a time so a burst gets no extra guesses, an address written in a disguised form can no longer reach the hub or other devices at home, the plugin proxy drops saved keys when a redirect leaves their site, and event names stay out of the hub's log.
- Calendar fixes: a yearly birthday no longer shows every month, a Daily calendar showing many days fills its later days, an event past midnight reads right in small pills, and disconnecting Google in one place no longer signs out Google everywhere else on the hub.
- Weather fixes: WeatherAPI uses the higher of its rain and snow chances, Moon Phase draws the crescent that matches its percentage, rain amounts use your units, and Full-Screen Weather says "No weather data" instead of loading forever.
- Photo fixes: iCloud, OneDrive and Immich backgrounds no longer rotate onto the picture already showing, a picture that has gone says so, uploads name every file they refuse and hold back videos the wall cannot play, and videos keep playing through a long slideshow.
- Chores, rewards and timers: two quick reward edits on the phone both save, quick taps on a ticket balance all count, a chore tick that does not save says so, and the ring timer no longer cuts off a five-step routine.
- Small fixes: the plugin checksum Copy button works, a display that has never checked in says Offline, Escape no longer closes the whole plugin store, the picture picker no longer deletes on one click, the phone's Send alert keeps an urgent alert up as long as the household set, "1 seconds ago" reads right, and the Discord invite link works again.
