## New in this beta

- Backups can include your pictures: choose none, only the ones your screens use, or the whole library, and restore from the zip the same way.
- Recipe book on the wall: browse your Mealie or saved recipes by category, search with an on-screen keyboard, and cook from a card that ticks off ingredients and stays awake for up to an hour. The phone can send any recipe to the wall.
- Touch walls get finger scrolling and look-ahead arrows: calendars, meal planners, grocery and to-do lists scroll with a finger, and calendars and meal planners step weeks or months ahead or back, then slide home a minute after the last touch.
- Weather Combined shows the UV index and sunrise and sunset, like the Current and Compact views.
- Clock and Date week numbers follow your language's week start, so a Monday-start household counts ISO weeks and a Sunday-start household counts the US way.
- Phone and editor requests give up after a set time and say the hub was out of reach, in your language, instead of hanging or quietly showing a saved change that never landed.
- Sign-ins and token refreshes ride out a short outage at Google, OneDrive or a plugin service instead of failing, and Google Calendar and OneDrive stay connected when a disconnect fails.
- Backup restore checks chores, rewards and meals by the same rules as a normal save, so a bad file is refused with a clear reason instead of being written.
- Meal planner fixes: the phone keeps the last Mealie plan while Mealie is down, a shopping list deleted in Mealie is reported as gone, a dinner with only a side leads with it, dishes are not offered after midnight, Undo after Clear Week keeps meals planned since, and a wrong or old Mealie address says plainly it cannot be used.
- Small fixes: a reward redeem says the same thing on the wall and the phone, the meal's color stripe stays on the current meal, a chosen accent color no longer draws a line across the flip clock, a typed rgb() color fills avatars and rings instead of painting nothing, Immich with a rejected key shows its setup card, switching Todoist accounts shows the new tasks right away, and half-written pictures or kiosk settings can no longer be left behind by a power cut.
- A batch of wording and layout tidy-ups across the editor, the phone and the wall.

## New

- Family notes board: write a note on your phone or in the editor and it shows on the wall with who it is from, in a new Notes module with twenty-two views, from a full board to a chalkboard or a neon sign.
- Calendar day notes: add up to three short notes to any day from the phone's Notes tab, and every calendar shows them on that day.
- Mealie support: connect your Mealie recipe library to plan meals from its recipes on the phone, in the editor and on the wall, with Mealie's meal plan and shopping list kept in step.
- Recipe book on the wall: browse your Mealie or saved recipes by category, search with an on-screen keyboard, and cook from a card that ticks off ingredients and stays awake for up to an hour. The phone can send any recipe to the wall.
- Recipe and Grocery List views for the meal planners: show the next recipe step by step on the wall, open any meal's recipe as a card with a QR code, and tick off groceries on a touch screen.
- Meals can have sides, desserts and drinks alongside the main dish, and every meal shows its total cooking time.
- Savings goals, chore steps and a ticket history: kids can save up for a reward, follow a chore one step at a time, see a celebration when the day's chores are done, and look back at every ticket earned and spent. Parents can give or take tickets with a reason.
- Backups can include your pictures: choose none, only the ones your screens use, or the whole library, and restore from the zip the same way.
- Touch walls get finger scrolling and look-ahead arrows: calendars, meal planners, grocery and to-do lists scroll with a finger, and calendars and meal planners step weeks or months ahead or back, then slide home a minute after the last touch.
- A dock for Display Control: one tile per screen to jump straight to it, with your own names, icons and colors, plus optional Sleep and Brightness buttons.
- Show a module on every screen: it stays put while the screens around it change.
- Whole screens can show or hide on conditions, the same way modules can, such as a screen that only appears when rain is on the way.

## Improved

- An Automatic rotation switch turns screen rotation off, for a display you move along by tapping instead.
- Meal planning: Undo after clearing a meal, changing a dish, Copy Last Week or Suggest; Suggest fills every dinner without repeats; the grocery list adds amounts together; and the phone's meal picker can search your meals.
- Weather Combined shows the UV index and sunrise and sunset, like the Current and Compact views.
- Clock and Date week numbers follow your language's week start, so a Monday-start household counts ISO weeks and a Sunday-start household counts the US way.
- Phone and editor requests give up after a set time and say the hub was out of reach, in your language, instead of hanging or quietly showing a saved change that never landed.
- Sign-ins and token refreshes ride out a short outage at Google, OneDrive or a plugin service instead of failing, and Google Calendar and OneDrive stay connected when a disconnect fails.
- Backup restore checks chores, rewards and meals by the same rules as a normal save, so a bad file is refused with a clear reason instead of being written.
- The living sky in Full-Screen Weather and the Rain Map run smoothly on a Raspberry Pi 4, and frosted cards drop their blur while a list scrolls.
- The wall rests while the display is asleep instead of drawing screens nobody can see, and the phone pauses its polling while its tab is hidden.
- The calendar says it is loading until its events arrive, instead of looking empty.
- Underlying libraries are updated past their latest security fixes.
- A batch of wording and layout tidy-ups across the editor, the phone and the wall.

## Fixed

- Rejoining a saved WiFi network works again on the newest Raspberry Pi OS, a saved password is kept when you rejoin, and the Pi asks before you forget the network you are connected through.
- Meal planner fixes: a quick double tap counts once on dishes and grocery items, the phone keeps the last Mealie plan while Mealie is down, a shopping list deleted in Mealie is reported as gone, a dinner with only a side leads with it, dishes are not offered after midnight, Undo after Clear Week keeps meals planned since, a wrong or old Mealie address says plainly it cannot be used, names on the wall's week grid are readable, the full-screen Week fills the screen, and Today moves on to tomorrow after the last meal.
- Pictures and the kiosk settings file are written whole or not at all, so a power cut can no longer leave a half-written photo or an empty settings file behind.
- Small fixes: clocks change right on the minute, dates show in the wall's language from the first moment, starter chores stay matched after a language switch, to-do renames in the editor are no longer undone by an older copy, a reward redeem says the same thing on the wall and the phone, the meal's color stripe stays on the current meal, a chosen accent color no longer draws a line across the flip clock, a typed rgb() color fills avatars and rings instead of painting nothing, Immich with a rejected key shows its setup card, and switching Todoist accounts shows the new tasks right away.
