## New

- Full-Screen Weather module, the fifth full-screen module. Five views: Panorama stacks the current conditions, a next-hour rain strip, a temperature curve, a 7-day outlook with range bars, and a row of stats; Almanac is a grid of instrument cards (sun arc, moon phase, wind, humidity, pressure, UV, visibility, and the next 12 hours); Ambient is a huge read for across the room; Week ahead gives every forecast day its own band; Hour by hour draws the next 24 hours as a timeline. Every view has a portrait and a landscape layout, picked from the display's shape. The background tints itself by the current conditions and the position of the sun, with falling rain and snow that can be switched off for older hardware; it uses the same themes as the other full-screen modules, follows your 12/24-hour setting and the display's time zone, and its text size and density controls work independently so large type never pushes the layout off the screen.
- Three new full-screen calendar views, taking it to eight. Family grid puts each person on a row and the week across the columns; Up next shows a hero card for the next event with lists of what comes later, what already happened, and tomorrow; Free time draws a busy-and-free track for each person and calls out when everyone is free at once.
- Calendar views got several new ways to configure what they show: assign each calendar source to a family member under Settings > Calendar, with unassigned sources landing on an Everyone row that counts as busy for the whole family; a rolling hour window for the schedule and day timeline views that slides with the clock instead of a fixed start and end hour, with a footer naming the window and today's earlier events; the full-screen calendar week list can show the day's planned meals and a chore progress row, pulled from the meal planner and chore chart; a schedule start anchor that begins at today, the start of the week, or the weekend; and weather placement in calendar views, in the header, on day headers, or next to individual events, instead of a single on/off switch.
- Six new full-screen themes, taking the shared list to twelve: Sandstone, Vellum, Bloom, Aurora, Obsidian, and Horizon, each with its own background gradients, accent colors, and event styles; the theme picker now shows a preview tile of each theme's background, accent, and event look instead of a name in a dropdown. Event and day rules for both calendar modules: match events by title, calendar, location, all-day, finished, or type and then hide them or change their color, fade, icon, or displayed title; match days by today, past, future, weekday, or whether they have events and then set a background, fade, outline, or badge; rules run in order, so the list itself is the priority. Birthdays and holidays now look like birthdays and holidays, with their own glyph instead of the usual color dot, "Birthday, turns 12" or "Holiday" instead of the generic all-day label, and bold name-first text with a warm tint in the month grid; works with iCloud contact birthdays, Google's Birthdays calendar, and the public holiday source. Multi-week calendar themes: the original banner look plus three modern grids (clean, minimal, and vivid) with corner day numbers, a today ring, weekend shading, stitched multi-day bars, and overflow chips; the month grid uses the same themes, and the per-cell event cap applies to the week, month, and multi-week grids.
- Calendar source legend: an optional list of which calendars are feeding the current view, at the top or bottom of either calendar module, showing only the sources that actually have events on screen. Calendar source health: if one calendar stops responding, the display keeps showing its last-good events with a "saved" marker instead of going blank, rings the failing source in the legend, and names it in the header; each source's row on the Calendar settings page says in plain language what went wrong and when it last worked. Calendars can now drive conditional visibility and text: seven new live values (next event title, start, time, minutes away, events today, busy now, and all-day today) are published to the shared state bus, so any module can show or hide itself based on what is on the calendar, even on displays with no calendar module.
- More calendar display detail: a countdown pill before an event starts and a progress bar while it's running on list rows, with a separate opt-in for all-day countdowns; keyword filters to include or exclude events by matching words in the title, plus a "Hide events you declined" option for Google calendars; a now line and past dimming in the daily view that fades finished events and rings whatever is running right now; a "Show Today's Finished Events" option for both agenda views so events that ended earlier today stay visible for the rest of the day; and multi-day timed events that now read correctly across days, with a real start time on the first day, an end time on the last, and all-day rows in between.
- Optional title strip on module cards: give any module a centered caption with its own size control, set in the Style panel.
- Per-module timezone for the Clock and Date modules: pick any timezone per module with a new searchable picker, or leave it blank to follow the display setting. The same picker now powers the Location settings page.
- Sky color theme for the sunrise and sunset circle view: a gradient ring through sunrise, noon, golden hour, sunset, and darkness, with scattered stars through the astronomical-dark window. Both themes now handle midnight sun and polar night.
- The homepage at homescreens.dev now walks through 22 real dashboard screenshots with descriptions and module lists, replacing the old rotating frame, and Discord community links were added to the README, the marketing site, and the docs.

## Improved

- Settings > Calendar was reorganized: grouped into where events come from, who is in the household, and what to show, with each calendar source carrying its own health badge on its row instead of a separate status list; calendar module settings are grouped into labelled sections in the same order for both calendar modules (view picker, What shows, the current view's own settings, Event rows, Look, Advanced looks, and Touch) so settings that belong to other views no longer sit interleaved with the ones you're editing; full-screen calendar settings now show only what applies to the current view, like the now-line toggle on time-grid views and weekend shading on the week list and agenda; and when a calendar source fails, the reason now reads in your own language rather than always in English.
- More detail from your weather source: UV index now comes through from Pirate Weather, Met Office, WeatherAPI, and Open-Meteo; pressure, dew point, and visibility from Pirate Weather; and OpenWeatherMap's hourly forecast now covers five days instead of 24 hours, fetched once per refresh instead of twice.
- Dark mode now covers scrollbars, dropdowns, and form controls, which previously stayed light against the dark app.
- The editor canvas zooms on fixed stops from 20% to 400% instead of drifting by a multiplier, and Ctrl+wheel moves exactly one stop per notch. Zoomed-in canvases can now scroll all the way to their left edge.
- The multi-week calendar grid got a visual pass: every day number sits in a shaded header tinted with the accent color, today keeps the stronger fill, and the gaps between day cells are wider.
- Custom empty-day wording and week or month separators in the calendar agenda views.
- Uploading a background image in a format the display cannot show now says so instead of failing quietly, and GIF and AVIF backgrounds are saved under their real file type.
- The documentation and module reference were brought back in line with the app: corrected accent color defaults, the full twelve-theme palette list, the calendar keyword filter, source legend, and rules, the new birthday and holiday event types, and the current API reference.
- Updated bundled dependencies to clear security advisories, and the packaged server download is considerably smaller.

## Fixed

- Google Calendar events failed to load on installs upgraded to 1.11.0-rc.1 because part of the Google client was missing from the packaged server. The package now ships intact.
- OpenWeatherMap users on metric units were seeing wind speeds in meters per second under a km/h label. Wind is now converted to km/h like every other source, so the numbers will look roughly three and a half times larger than before. The new numbers are the correct ones.
- The clock, date, year progress, multi-month, and text modules could report a mismatch and briefly render the server's time when a display's own clock had drifted; they now settle on the display's time cleanly.
- Full-screen calendar layout fixes: overlapping events in the Free time view no longer print their titles on top of each other, the first and last hour labels sit inside the schedule and day timeline grids instead of being sliced at the edge, an event crossing a zone boundary no longer paints over the zone label, a standalone event keeps its full column width when a different part of the day is crowded, the family grid fills the screen in portrait, the agenda fades at its edge instead of cutting the last card in half, and large title text no longer overlaps the weather badges in the header and family grid day headers.
- The Up next view now lists events happening right now under their own "Now" heading instead of filing them under "Earlier today", uses two columns in landscape so tomorrow is no longer cut off the bottom, and can show per-event weather like the other views.
- The editor's weather source table listed Met Office as having no optional fields; it now shows what Met Office actually provides.
- The midnight label in the full-screen calendar's hour gutter read "12 PM" instead of "12 AM".
- Calendar setups using only iCloud calendars did not fetch any events at all, and iCloud calendars were missing from the editor's source filter.
- The fullscreen calendar placed events using the computer's clock instead of the display's timezone, so events could land on the wrong day or at the wrong position in every one of its five views. Events crossing a daylight-saving change now keep their real duration.
- Calendar polish across both modules: 24-hour clock labels, weekend shading defaults, holiday glyphs, the "saved" suffix on stale rows, screen-reader labels on event rows, and events needlessly redrawing every minute on displays.
- The sun glow on the sunrise and sunset circle view stayed lit all night; it now turns off between sunset and sunrise, and the marker dims to match.
- Opening the Calendar settings tab could crash the editor when no Google calendars were configured.
- The fullscreen calendar's Density setting showed a raw internal name instead of its label.
- Multi-week grid fixes: the month abbreviation and day number no longer run together, day numbers and today's badge pick a readable text color against light accent colors, and weekday labels stay lined up with their columns when week numbers are shown.
- Embedded web pages in the iframe module no longer change appearance based on the app's theme, and the meal planner's time inputs now follow the app theme instead of being locked to dark.
- The calendar status page could be read without signing in, exposing your calendar names and any error text from your calendar provider. It now requires the same access as the rest of the display.
