## New

- Family notes board: write a note on your phone or in the editor and it shows on the wall with who it is from, in a new Notes module with twenty-two views, from a full board to a chalkboard or a neon sign.
- Calendar day notes: add up to three short notes to any day from the phone's Notes tab, and every calendar shows them on that day.
- Mealie support: connect your Mealie recipe library to plan meals from its recipes on the phone, in the editor and on the wall, with Mealie's meal plan and shopping list kept in step.
- Recipe and Grocery List views for the meal planners: show the next recipe step by step on the wall, open any meal's recipe as a card with a QR code, and tick off groceries on a touch screen.
- Meals can have sides, desserts and drinks alongside the main dish, and every meal shows its total cooking time.
- Savings goals, chore steps and a ticket history: kids can save up for a reward, follow a chore one step at a time, see a celebration when the day's chores are done, and look back at every ticket earned and spent. Parents can give or take tickets with a reason.
- A dock for Display Control: one tile per screen to jump straight to it, with your own names, icons and colors, plus optional Sleep and Brightness buttons.
- Show a module on every screen: it stays put while the screens around it change.
- Whole screens can show or hide on conditions, the same way modules can, such as a screen that only appears when rain is on the way.

## Improved

- An Automatic rotation switch turns screen rotation off, for a display you move along by tapping instead.
- Meal planning: Undo after clearing a meal, changing a dish, Copy Last Week or Suggest; Suggest fills every dinner without repeats; the grocery list adds amounts together; and the phone's meal picker can search your meals.
- The living sky in Full-Screen Weather and the Rain Map run smoothly on a Raspberry Pi 4.
- The wall rests while the display is asleep instead of drawing screens nobody can see.
- The calendar says it is loading until its events arrive, instead of looking empty.
- A batch of wording and layout tidy-ups across the editor, the phone and the wall.

## Fixed

- Rejoining a saved WiFi network works again on the newest Raspberry Pi OS, a saved password is kept when you rejoin, and the Pi asks before you forget the network you are connected through.
- Meal planner fixes: a quick double tap counts once on dishes and grocery items, names on the wall's week grid are readable, the full-screen Week fills the screen, and Today moves on to tomorrow after the last meal.
- Small fixes: clocks change right on the minute, dates show in the wall's language from the first moment, starter chores stay matched after a language switch, and to-do renames in the editor are no longer undone by an older copy.
