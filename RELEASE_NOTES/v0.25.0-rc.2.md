## New

- Immich integration — browse and display photos from your self-hosted Immich library in slideshows, the fullscreen photo viewer, and background rotation. Filter by album, person, or favorites. Pick individual Immich photos as screen backgrounds from a new Immich tab in the background picker.

## Improved

- Redesigned Integrations settings page with card-based layout, branded service icons, connection status badges, and category groupings for a cleaner overview of all connected services.

## Fixed

- WiFi reliability on Raspberry Pi — apply official Raspberry Pi Foundation driver fixes for the BCM43455 chip to prevent disconnections and 5GHz crashes, and reserve kernel memory for network buffers under load.
