## New

- Six new Weather views: Temperature ribbon, Week ahead, Hour by hour, Stats, Sun and moon, and Written forecast. The Current and Compact views can also show the UV index and sunrise and sunset.
- Forecast and Conditions views for Full-Screen Weather, with a living sky of drifting clouds, rain, snow, fog, stars and the moon at its real phase. Choose which cards show and put them in any order.
- Starter chore list: pick from 27 ready-made chores for mornings, after school, evenings and once a week, on the phone or in the editor, and choose who gets them in one step.
- Looks for the Text module: change the text color, background, border or icon, show different words, or fade the module when a live value changes, such as a Home Assistant sensor.
- Modules can show or hide with the weather, using the condition, the temperature, the chance of rain or snow, or an active warning from Pirate Weather or NOAA. The Rain Map has a one-click "Only show when rain is likely".
- Google Calendar and Google Photos sign-in works out of the box with Home Screens' own Google app. A household that set up its own Google app can keep it or switch with one toggle.

## Improved

- Sports Scores Cards and List views page through every game instead of cutting some off, and a new Next page every setting sets how fast the pages turn.
- Weather settings say how far your weather source forecasts, in days and in hours, with a one-click switch to Open-Meteo when you ask for more days than it has, and show only the options the chosen view uses, in named groups. Full-Screen Weather can use its own weather source.
- The Pi keeps its web browser and other display programs up to date whenever Home Screens installs or updates, and the Stats page shows their versions.
- The update window shows it is still working during a long, quiet step: a running clock beside the step, a note when nothing new has come in for a while, and which display programs it is downloading and installing.
- Dragging and resizing modules in the editor is smoother, and dropping a module back where it was no longer counts as a change.
- The docs and README cover everything new in 1.14, homescreens.dev has a plugin directory to browse, search and submit plugins, and the privacy policy explains how your Google data is protected.
- Underlying libraries are updated past their latest security fixes, including the web framework and the image library.
- A batch of wording and layout tidy-ups across the editor and the wall.

## Fixed

- The wall no longer freezes after a network blip: every request it makes now gives up after a set time instead of waiting forever.
- An update that starts but then answers with errors no longer throws away the way back to the previous version.
- Display sleep fixes: a monitor that drops its connection in standby stays dark instead of lighting up every ten seconds, and a screen switching on no longer wakes the display by itself.
- Weather fixes: Open-Meteo pressure no longer reads too low for homes above sea level, the WeatherAPI hour strip's Now is the current hour instead of the next one, WeatherAPI no longer shows a sun on evening hours, night hours shade as one band in hourly views, the Almanac moon no longer shows lit slivers at its tips, and warnings shared with plugins carry their real end time instead of a date in 1970.
- Small fixes: picking a background color for an Icon, Text or QR Code module now shows the color instead of staying see-through, picking an Unsplash background turns off background rotation from every place you can pick one, the editor no longer warns that nothing sends a live value when a plugin does send it, and updating a Pi no longer leaves a spare copy of your photos and settings inside the app folder.
