## New

- News module rebuilt around multiple feeds, plus a new Full-Screen News module. Add as many RSS, Atom, or JSON feeds as you like, or pick from 98 curated presets in all seven languages. Built-in sources cover local news for your location, topic searches, YouTube channels, and subreddits. Each feed gets a label and colour, and stories can be filtered by age, blocked or required words, and duplicates. The card module adds a Cards photo grid view, a paged list, ticker separators with touch pause, source and age lines, "just in" marks, and tap to open a QR code or summary. Full-Screen News offers Story and Front page views in both orientations. Existing news modules carry their single feed over automatically.
- OneDrive is a photo source for both photo modules, a community contribution. Sign in with a code from your Microsoft account, browse to a folder in the editor, and the slideshow pulls photos from that folder and every subfolder. Photos are served through Home Screens, so the display never needs its own Microsoft sign-in.
- Stock ticker tiles can chart today, the past week, or both side by side, with a Classic or Shaded theme and optional range captions. A new Single view dedicates a tile to one symbol with a large price, change, and full-tile chart.
- Rain map now reads radar from LibreWXR after RainViewer's free service shut down. A Radar server field under Settings > Weather points it at a self-hosted copy; blank means the public server. Every zoom level on the slider is available.
- Clock module: Hour format (follow the display, 12-hour, or 24-hour; new clocks follow the display), Alignment and Vertical align settings, a Size setting (Fits the box or Fixed), and a Show AM/PM toggle on the Minimal view.
- Multi-month calendar: today marker styles (filled, square, outline, underline, coloured number, none) with an accent colour, an option to hide the current month's heading, and a Fill the space toggle so a large card is filled with larger type. The multi-week calendar can now show as few as two weeks.
- Calendar grid views have a Date text size slider that scales day names, day numbers, and week numbers independently of the event text.
- Text size is one control on every module, read as a percent of the module's normal size. Modules that fit their text to their box can now be made smaller than they fit as well as larger, down to 10 percent. Existing settings render exactly as before.
- Show Title toggle on every module with a built-in header (traffic, garbage day, history, news, todo, Todoist, weather, chore chart, meal planner, and the full-screen meal planner).
- Module schedules are drawn as a week strip that shows exactly when the module is on, with the hidden hours outlined when Hide during these hours is set. A schedule can now be one stretch that runs several days, dragged to length on the strip. The Time of day visibility condition uses the same picture.
- Full-Screen Weather adds a Wide strip view: Panorama's parts laid side by side for a module across the foot of a landscape screen.
- Full-screen chore chart adds a By person layout with one section per member, a week progress option (chips, strip, grid, or off), and a name under each person's dot when there is room.
- Display Control adds an Arrows-only layout with just Previous and Next, sizes itself to its box, and targets its own display by default.
- Editor: screen search with Cmd/Ctrl+K finds screens by name or the modules they hold across every display. A Preview button opens the screen being edited in a new tab with rotation held. Keyboard shortcuts: arrow keys nudge the selected module, Delete removes it, Escape deselects, Cmd+D duplicates. Modules get a right-click menu, alignment guides that snap to neighbours, and Duplicate for modules and screens. Import a layout file straight from the + menu on the screen tabs.
- Editor workspace: the module list and settings panel collapse to icon rails, eight backgrounds ship with Home Screens, every module has a plain description and search keywords, and the editor asks before adding a full-screen module to a busy screen.
- Save conflict handling: when someone else has saved since you loaded, the editor offers to load their changes or keep yours instead of silently overwriting, and an idle editor notices outside changes.
- Settings > On your phone explains the family remote and the kids' chores page side by side with scannable codes and a printable sheet. The phone chips in the chore, meal, and photo settings open a QR dialog.
- First run explains itself: a display with no content shows a watermark with the editor address, the empty editor offers a template and a first-run checklist, the root URL on a phone opens a launcher for the family remote, display, and kids' chores, and location-bound modules link straight to the Location page.
- Opt-in credential backup: a backup can now include API keys, calendar passwords, Google and OneDrive sign-ins, and the editor password, sealed with a password of your choosing. Restoring without the password keeps everything else.
- Calendar links and weather keys are checked before they are saved. A bad calendar link or rejected key stays in the form with the reason and a Save anyway option.
- System settings show full release notes in a dialog, one row per release, and can roll back to any of the last three versions.
- Family remote: sleep, wake, next, previous, and brightness confirm from the display or revert with a message naming it. Displays report their brightness, on-screen alerts, and running timer. Restart and reboot ask first and show a reconnecting banner. Send Alert offers Clear alerts with the on-screen count.
- Kids' chores page: un-check a finished chore with press and hold, a Yesterday/Today toggle, the page remembers which kid was picked per device, and Rewards spend only for that kid.
- A forgotten editor password can be reset with a script on the Pi, linked from the login page and the Security settings. The login allowance is 10 tries before a pause.
- Icon picker for calendar event icons, day badges, and the text module prefix, with a curated emoji tab and the full Font Awesome catalog.
- Displays that run on the same Pi as Home Screens now print the hub's network address on the setup watermark, the empty photo screen, and the chores and meals empty states, instead of localhost.
- Cross-origin writes to the API are refused, protecting an install with no editor password from a page on another site. Reverse proxies that rewrite the host can set HS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS.
- Docs rebuilt around the family's tasks: Start here, Set up your content, Customize, More displays, Help, and Reference, with guides for what to buy, your first screen, weather, calendars, chores and rewards, meals, and plugins, plus 36 real screenshots. Docs search answers the questions people type, and the homepage showcase has a landscape view.

## Improved

- Modules size their type to their box again. Weather views had been rendering at the default size in whatever box they had, and the news list could get stuck showing every row clipped. Text is measured again once the real font loads, so a Pi no longer lays out text in a fallback face.
- Style controls reach every module they are offered to. Sports, standings, the analog clock face, and divider lines follow the text colour; light cards keep their rules, rings, and placeholders; the sticky note hides the colour controls it cannot use; Display Control hides the Style section entirely.
- The household 12/24-hour setting now reaches sunrise and sunset, moon phase, the full-screen photo clock, Todoist due times, and the weather hourly, combined, and alerts views. Sunrise and moon phase also format in the display's language.
- The display, family remote, and kids' page render in the app's own font instead of the system fallback, which on a Pi was Liberation Sans with no light weights. Georgia gets a matching bundled substitute so the font picker looks the same on the display.
- Chore chart card fits its day to its box instead of cutting off rows, and both chore charts say "N more below" when a household is too big for the card. The Board view wraps member columns into balanced rows, Compact collapses per-person columns into one pill when they would crowd out the chore name, and members with no chores this week are left out of every view.
- Full-screen chore chart rebuilt for big families and small boxes: measured rows, two-column portrait and four-column landscape packing, chore names that follow the typography size, and a rewards store on the same layout. The star chart fits its box and names who is below the fold.
- Full-screen meal planner Today, Menu board, and Next meal views fill the canvas. Meal week views drop unused slot columns and show quiet empty slots.
- Alerts are wider with larger titles. Urgent alerts are a full-width bar that wakes a sleeping display and puts it back to sleep when dismissed.
- Unconfigured modules show the same placeholder everywhere: icon, name, and one plain hint. Module defaults are sized to their content.
- The wall shows a quiet setup card linking to the right settings page when a key or sign-in is missing, and a "not updating" line when a source fails, instead of raw error text or HTTP codes. Weather says "Getting the forecast" before its first payload.
- Sports kickoff times show in the display's own timezone as Today, Tomorrow, or a weekday and date.
- The rotation progress line is centred under the dot row and stays pinned to the dwell clock. Screen pagination collapses past ten screens to a counter with arrows.
- Google Calendar pages through every event instead of stopping at 250, the Max Events setting is gone in favour of a fixed safety cap, and views that dim past events fetch from the start of today. Calendar feeds with non-standard time zone names like MDT or CST now load, with daylight saving respected.
- Calendar events stay on the wall through a midnight rollover during an outage, and a rescheduled appointment no longer shows at both its old and new times.
- Photo slideshows keep a batch until the pass completes, so a periodic reshuffle never swaps the current slide early. Crossfades wait for the incoming photo to load and skip one whose download fails. The screen background holds the previous image while the next loads.
- Rain map tiles are cached across screens and instances, a cold start plays one forward sweep, and a frame with a failed tile is held out of the loop and retried instead of playing with a hole.
- Fullscreen weather columns no longer overlap at large typography sizes, the temperature ribbon keeps its hour labels off the curve, and today's high and low are reconciled with the current reading so an evening feed no longer shows the night's low as today's high.
- Multi-month calendar uses a fixed six-week grid that fits its box, so six months side by side no longer run their columns together.
- The remote badges a display Offline with its last-seen time from the hub, tells never-connected displays apart from waiting ones, and hides the screen dot strip past 12 screens on a phone. Chores, Meals, and Photos tabs stay in the bar and explain what is missing.
- Family remote forms on a phone: the chore form leads with name, tickets, and frequency with Save and Delete in a sticky footer; thumb-sized tap targets on reward tabs, ticket buttons, and timer and meal chips; each timer look shows a small drawing of what it puts on the wall; every form asks before discarding unsaved edits; tapping a planned meal opens the picker instead of clearing it.
- Every settings page has a title, breadcrumb, and one-line purpose, autosaving pages say so, sidebar groups collapse, and each page links to its docs. Security leads with what the password covers. System and Status hide developer details behind Show advanced options. Profiles are named on creation and show their screens and schedule while collapsed.
- Plain language pass over the display, login, remote, and module strings: hub becomes Home Screens or the main Pi, adopt becomes add, widget becomes module.
- Weather providers are named the same way on the Weather page, the API keys page, and the wall's setup card. A keyed default provider with no key is called out at the top of the Weather page with a one-click switch to Open-Meteo.
- The displays fleet grid shows which screen each display is on. Rotation progress and the setup watermark can be overridden per display.
- The rotation interval sliders go up to 600 seconds, and a short per-screen duration saves as typed with a warning instead of being clamped to 10 seconds.
- Editor settings sidebar has GitHub and Discord links below Help and docs.
- Imported layout files are migrated to the current schema before they are merged, so an old export still loads.
- The Location page leads with the town, then timezone, then a collapsed clock check, with plain-language help for the timezone and detect button.
- Updated bundled dependencies to clear security advisories.

## Fixed

- The Pi image never started its kiosk: the login console was left disabled and the kiosk user's home directory was owned by root. Both are fixed and checked during the image build.
- The release download shipped no starting configuration, so the Pi image's first boot could not finish. The image now seeds its own.
- A fresh install no longer starts on a weather provider that needs an API key.
- The history module failed to load because the Wikimedia service rate-limited requests. It now sends a proper identifier, stops retrying, and keeps the last good events.
- Modules lost their selection at the end of a resize drag. Resized or moved modules can no longer be pushed off the canvas.
- Animated settings sections scrolled under the pointer mid-animation, so a click could land on the wrong row.
- Undo and redo no longer fire on the canvas while a dialog or context menu is open.
- Edits typed into a settings form while a save conflict was waiting are kept when you pick Keep mine.
- The login page, family remote, and kids' page briefly showed raw text keys instead of words while loading.
- Chore chart Board view collapsed every member into one row of truncated columns on a heavy day.
- The remote's Photos tab showed a permanent empty state for households using the windowed photo slideshow.
- The star chart marked future days as missed in the evening, and a chore-free day is no longer counted as missed.
- Fullscreen calendar week titles that crossed a month or year were wrong.
- Two-photo slideshows stopped advancing when the same photo loaded twice, and every slide rotation briefly flashed the photo from two advances back.
- The full-screen photo theme override painted its letterbox bars and clock overlay black regardless of theme.
- A schedule with equal start and end times saved as an empty window instead of a full day, and an inverted schedule lost its invert on edit.
- An urgent alert that outlived the overnight sleep window left the display awake afterward.
- Alerts and timers no longer disappear on a display whose screens are all empty.
- A calendar showing a rolling window could show can't load at midnight during an outage.
- Sunrise and sunset labels did not grow with the text size setting.
- The editor preview of a timer routine no longer shows or drives the live timer session.
- The remote's profile switcher fell back to the page-load profile after a display reported none.
- Weather providers reported a missing hourly temperature as 0 degrees.
- The kids' Manage view was reachable without a grown-up session.
- The pressure sparkline on the weather almanac claimed most of a short card and clipped the readout row.
- The Show Title toggle appeared on news views that draw no heading.
- The "&check;" text in the chore Today view is a real check mark.
- The air quality setup card pointed at the wrong settings page for its key.
- The on-screen arrows on the kiosk pagination bar are translated.
- Backup and credential errors on the phone read as a sentence instead of a machine code.
- A sign-in refresh started before a disconnect could no longer overwrite the newer grant.
- Deleting the only reward was refused by an empty-list guard.
- Installing Home Screens on a Pi no longer overwrites household font settings; the font aliases are installed as their own file.
