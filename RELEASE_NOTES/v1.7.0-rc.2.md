## New

- Plugins can now connect to services that require signing in. Plugins declare how they connect, and Home Screens handles the sign-in flow, keeps you signed in automatically, and shows a Connection panel in the editor with connect and disconnect controls (including a guided email, password, and one-time-code flow for Garmin Connect). Connections survive upgrades and are removed when a plugin is uninstalled.
- Photo slideshows now accept the new-style iCloud shared album links (photos.icloud.com/shared/album), with both photos and videos supported. Older shared album links keep working alongside them.

## Fixed

- Videos from iCloud and other remote slideshow sources were blocked from playing; they now play normally.
- Display crash when a video download was interrupted mid-stream, which happened routinely during kiosk video playback.
