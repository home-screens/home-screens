## New

- Family notes board: write a note on your phone or in the editor and it shows on the wall with who it is from, in a new Notes module with twenty-two looks, from a full board to a neon sign.
- Calendar day notes: add up to three short notes to any day from the phone, and every calendar shows them on that day.
- Mealie support: plan meals from your Mealie recipe library on the phone, in the editor and on the wall, with Mealie's meal plan and shopping list kept in step.
- Recipe book on the wall: browse and search your recipes on a touch screen, then cook from a card that ticks off ingredients and keeps the screen awake. The phone can send any recipe to the wall.
- Recipe and Grocery List views for the meal planners, so the wall can walk through tonight's recipe step by step or hold a grocery list you tick off with a finger.
- Family meal ratings: everyone gives dinner their own stars from the wall or the phone, and a meal's rating is what the family thinks of it. Thanks to o1hitman1o for the contribution.
- Heart your favorite recipes from the phone, the wall and the editor.
- Meals can have sides, desserts and drinks alongside the main dish, and every meal shows its total cooking time.
- Savings goals, chore steps and a ticket history, so kids can save up for a reward, follow a chore one step at a time and look back at every ticket earned and spent.
- Open buttons: add a button under any module that opens a chosen screen on the wall, such as the recipe book from the meal planner, and that screen stays out of the rotation.
- Museum paintings: a new Art photo source with over 4,000 public-domain paintings that ship with Home Screens, hung in a frame or mat with a museum label. Thanks to Andoramb for the contribution.
- Backgrounds can rotate through several photo sources at once, mixing the built-in walls, your own library, Immich, Unsplash, iCloud, NASA and museum paintings on one screen. Thanks to Andoramb for the contribution.
- One Image Library for every spot that holds a picture, with a tab for each photo source.
- Background pictures fade from one to the next, and each screen gets brightness, contrast, saturation and warmth sliders.
- Evening light warms and dims any background from sunset until morning, and art mode hides a screen's modules so only the picture shows.
- Burn-in protection: an optional setting, per display, that shifts the whole wall a few pixels once a minute.
- Backups can include your pictures: none, only the ones your screens use, or the whole library.
- Touch walls get finger scrolling for calendars, meal planners and lists, plus arrows to look a week or month ahead.
- A dock for Display Control: one tile per screen to jump straight to it, with your own names, icons and colors.
- Show a module on every screen, where it stays put while the screens around it change.
- Whole screens can show or hide on conditions, the same way modules can, such as a screen that only appears when rain is on the way.

## Improved

- Calendar gets a "Title only" look for all-day events, so each one takes a single line.
- An Automatic rotation switch turns screen rotation off, for a display you move along by tapping instead.
- Meal planning: Undo after clearing or changing a meal, Suggest fills every dinner without repeats, the grocery list adds amounts together, and the phone's meal picker can search.
- The wall's recipe book keeps up with Mealie, showing new recipes and hearts within about a minute, and search finds short words by name.
- Photo source tidy-ups: Slideshow, Full-screen Photo and backgrounds share the same settings, NASA's Picture of the Day no longer needs a key, Immich lists shared albums and can leave people out, and the photographer credit is a quiet caption.
- Rotating backgrounds are clearer to set up in the editor: every picked picture is listed, and a source that finds nothing says why.
- The update window stays open through a dropped connection, and a failed download says why.
- Weather Combined shows the UV index and sunrise and sunset, like the Current and Compact views.
- Clock and Date week numbers follow your language's week start.
- Steadier through outages: phone and editor requests give up after a set time and say the hub was out of reach, and sign-ins ride out a short outage at Google, OneDrive or a plugin service.
- Backup restore checks chores, rewards and meals by the same rules as a normal save, so a bad file is refused with a clear reason.
- Lighter on a Raspberry Pi: the living sky and Rain Map run smoothly on a Pi 4, frosted cards drop their blur while a list scrolls, the wall rests while the display is asleep, and the phone pauses while its tab is hidden.
- The calendar says it is loading until its events arrive, instead of looking empty.
- For plugin makers: a plugin can start full-screen when added, and the editor's Preview window shows your development build.
- Underlying libraries are updated past their latest security fixes.
- A batch of wording and layout tidy-ups across the editor, the phone and the wall.

## Fixed

- Rejoining a saved WiFi network works again on the newest Raspberry Pi OS, a saved password is kept when you rejoin, and the Pi asks before you forget the network you are connected through.
- Pictures and the kiosk settings file are written whole or not at all, so a power cut can no longer leave a half-written photo or an empty settings file behind.
- iCloud shared album fixes: albums no longer come back empty, a slow album gets a second try, albums are remembered across a restart, slides no longer go blank, and newer album links work as rotating backgrounds.
- Meal planner fixes: a quick double tap counts once, the phone keeps the last Mealie plan while Mealie is down, Undo after Clear Week keeps meals planned since, a wrong Mealie address says plainly it cannot be used, the meal's color stripe stays on the current meal, the full-screen planner fits the screen, and Today moves on to tomorrow after the last meal.
- Photo fixes: Immich with a rejected key shows its setup card, an Immich key with limited access shows its filters and names what is missing, a photo the browser cannot show is skipped instead of leaving a gap, and Refresh now says which source did not answer.
- Small fixes: clocks change right on the minute, dates show in the wall's language from the first moment, starter chores stay matched after a language switch, to-do renames in the editor are no longer undone, a reward redeem says the same thing on the wall and the phone, a chosen accent color no longer draws a line across the flip clock, a member color typed in by hand fills avatars and rings, switching Todoist accounts shows the new tasks right away, a new module bigger than the screen is shrunk to fit, the Display Control dock fades buttons for screens a display does not have, and dragging across days in a time condition picks the day under the pointer.
