## Improved

- Word of the Day now rotates through 732 curated words per language, enough for two full years without a repeat. When a cycle ends, the words reshuffle into a fresh order for the next one.
- Every supported language (English, German, French, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese, and Danish) gets its own hand-picked Word of the Day list with authentic local vocabulary, not translations of the English words.
- The Visibility section in the module settings panel now starts collapsed, keeping the panel tidier for modules with lots of options.
